## 2.1 What the roster demands (`mister_fit.md`)

- [MSV-1] **The demand figures are merged-m6's — quote them WITH their freeze.** PRG live to `PRG:0x4D10F3` plus the 30-byte facing-alias pin at `PRG:0x5FFF00` (deficit `0xD10F4` = 836 KB against a flat 4 MB); QSound extension 918 KB in DSP banks `0x80-0x8E`; group C = 16 MB of members. The four CEILINGS (`0xEE73`, `0xFFDB`, `0x8E57F0`, `0x5FFF1E`) are RE-DERIVED from the current freeze by `tests/audit_mister_map_fit.sh` on every run; the wide_ext HIGH-WATER MARK `0x4D10F3` is NOT gate-frozen and merged-m9 shifted the extension.
- [MSV-2] **The roster's art is 13x every blank tile in vanilla's 32 MB put together** (6.39 MB of live group-C tiles vs 0.49 MB blank, and blank is only an upper bound). There is no fit-inside-stock variant and no tenant-dropping variant (Pyron's band alone is 3.5x the blank total): a MiSTer build of this roster REQUIRES a GFX map wider than 32 MB.
- [MSV-3] **Two counts of "group-C live codes" circulate and are different quantities**: 52,347 codes / 6.39 MB from the as-built WRITE SET (`mister_fit.md` §3) vs 45,736 + 6,245 from the NON-BLANK census of the shipped members (`mister_map.md` §1, `audit_mister_map_fit`) — 366 tiles the build writes as all-`00`/`FF`. Neither governs placement ([MSC-22]).
- [MSV-4] **The frozen extents are what a moved extent has to be re-frozen AGAINST, deliberately.** Tenant art may grow freely inside the existing 16 MB (a code above `0xEE73` or `0xFFDB` overflows nothing), and `audit_mister_map_fit` still goes RED on purpose — re-derive, re-freeze, never absorb.

## 2.2 The placement map (`mister_map.md` §5)

- [MSV-5] **The map, in bytes**: bank 0 — PRG 6 MB at 0, VRAM `0x600000`, ORAM `0x640000`, work RAM `0x648000`, Z80 `0x658000`, QSound PCM-high `0x6E0000` (1 MB window), group-C obj bank 5 `0x7E0000` (8 MB region), **131,072 B free**; bank 1 — PCM-low 8 MB at 0, group-C obj bank 4 `0x800000`, **EXACTLY FULL**; banks 2 and 3 untouched to the byte. Total reserved 63.875 MB of 64: **slack 0.125 MB, all of it in bank 0** (the 0.708 MB once claimed sized the group-C banks by footprint and is RETRACTED).
- [MSV-6] **QSound is SPLIT across two banks on `pcm_addr[23]`** — stock DSP banks `0x00-0x7F` stay at bank 1 offset 0 byte-identical to stock, banks `0x80+` (exactly what the profile added) go to bank 0. Forced twice over: QSound whole in bank 1 overflows by 0.9375 MB best case, and a slot cannot exceed 8 MB anyway ([MSC-19]). The high window is masked to its 1 MB on BOTH sides; `vsw.22m` is simply not mapped.
- [MSV-7] **Group C is one obj bank per SDRAM bank, and the assignment is deliberate**: obj bank **4** (the three fighter bands — in-match traffic) → bank 1, whose headroom was measured; obj bank **5** (select/wheel art, cold in a match) → bank 0, so its load lands on the select screen.
- [MSV-8] **The QSound trim is MANDATORY, not an optimisation**: mapped verbatim the WIDE `.rom` is 73,670,720 B, past the 26-bit `ioctl_addr`, with a firmware start word silently WRAPPED to 6400. Placed at 8.9375 MB (`0x8F0000`, banks `0x00-0x8E`) it is **66,265,152 B** with header words **6144 / 6400 / 15552 / 64704**, every region 1 KiB-aligned; `test_mister_mra_map` holds it byte for byte.
- [MSV-9] **Bank 0 needs SEVEN streams, and upstream's `ram1_Nslots` family stops at five**: `jtframe_ram1_7slots.v` (ruled option A, 2026-08-23) is a mechanical sibling of `ram1_5slots.v`, placed with its family and pulled by `cores/cps2w/cfg/game.yaml` alone ([MSC-3]).
- [MSV-10] **The load is MEASURED on the WIDE image and it is GO — with the caveat stated**: bank 0 runs 40,717 accesses/frame through the select screen (32.9% of ceiling; the redirect costs ~1,000/frame, ~2.5%), whole-run peak 54,363 (43.9%); bank 1 peaks at 15,496 (12.5%) with the fighter art sharing it with PCM; zero `SDRAM reads clashed`. Bank 1's figure rests on ONE replay, ONE tenant, one opponent.
- [MSV-11] **What cannot grow: the group-C ROMSET REGION.** A fifth group-C member or anything past 16 MB overflows immediately — bank 1 has zero free and bank 0 has 131,072 B — and there is nowhere for the excess to go.
- [MSV-12] **On `cps2w` work RAM is SDRAM bank 0 byte `0x648000`; `0x600000` — the reference core's work RAM — is VRAM there.** `tools/run_sim_jtcps2.sh` selects the constant from `--core` and prints it; the stale one turned `test_mister_wide_inert` red 101 frames of 101 with the RTL innocent ([MSC-41]).
- [MSV-13] **The `$400000-$40000F` reservation is load-bearing THREE ways**: ROM on `jtcps2w`, ROM-shadowed on FBNeo, readable registers on MAME — a divergence unobservable only because nothing may live there. The allocator honours it (`wide_ext` starts at `0x400010`).
- [MSV-14] **The synthesis cannot go stale silently**: `tools/mk_mister_page.py --check` re-derives every figure `mister_core.md` states (17 checks) from the constants the gates freeze, on every `run_all_static`; the rendered page is never committed.

## 2.3 Catalogue, MRA and bundle generation

- [MSV-15] **The `vsavjw` catalogue entry is `vsavj`'s VERBATIM except `sourcefile="capcom/cps2w.cpp"` (the profile gate, [MSC-13]), the description and the ROM map**, and it is GENERATED from the built zip by `tools/gen_vsavjw_xml.py` because members resolve by CRC ([MSC-64]). `gen_vsavjw_xml.py <zip> --check emu/jtcores/doc/mame.xml` says whether the fork's entry is the current build's.
- [MSV-16] **EVERY romset re-freeze has a MiSTer TAIL, and it is triggered from OUTSIDE MiSTer**: a rebuild that moves one CRC needs a new fork commit (`doc/mame.xml`), a new patch `NNNN` in `emu/jtcores-patches/`, a pin bump, and regenerated MRAs; the tail is EMPTY only when `gen_vsavjw_xml.py --check` is ok on the new build (packaging-only freezes: merged-m10) AND the `.rbf` is unchanged. It was skipped at 14z-110 and 14z-110b — budget it in the freeze ritual, not after.
- [MSV-17] **The QSound extension is its OWN region `qsoundw`**, `{ name="qsoundw", skip=true }` for every other set and a single-part `parts=` window of `length=0x0F0000` for `vsavjw` with `map="12"` spelled out ([MSC-66]); `"qsoundw"` sits in `order` right after `"qsound"` so the RTL sees one 8.9375 MB region.
- [MSV-18] **ONE-ZIP PACKAGING since 14z-112**: the four patched group-A members `vm3.13m/15m/17m/19m` live INSIDE `vsavjw.zip`, no `vsav.zip` is packed, and BOTH legs use the PRISTINE dump — so one SD card carries this profile AND stock Vampire Savior (field-confirmed 2026-08-28). Before it, a patched parent in `games/mame/vsav.zip` gave a stock MRA wrong art SILENTLY.
- [MSV-19] **`tools/mister_mra.sh` is the only way to make MRAs and `.rom`s**: a scratch clone, a PRIVATE `$HOME` per run ([MSC-65]), `--wide <build>` for the WIDE leg, `--no-rom` for the ROM-free mode, `--toml`/`--xml` to perturb the mapping for a control; `.rom` out-dirs inside the repo are refused (rule 7).
- [MSV-20] **The MiSTer release layer is `release/<name>/mister/`** (`release_format.md`, since merged-m10): the WIDE and `[STOCK CONTROL]` MRAs, `jtcps2w.rbf` hash-verified from `release/bitstreams/<CURRENT>/`, `BITSTREAM.txt`, `MISTER.md`, produced by `tools/package_release_platforms.py` and locked by `test_release_roundtrip` §4. Every version releases every platform, even when only one changed.
- [MSV-21] **Every MRA part must resolve against the exact zips the card carries**: WIDE 31 of 31, STOCK CONTROL 22 of 22 against the pristine dump (`tools/check_mra_parts.py`, `tests/test_mra_parts.sh`). An unresolved part is `0xFF`-filled, not refused ([MSC-69]) — the 14z-108 bundle would have "run" the control with 8 parts missing.
- [MSV-22] **The `[STOCK CONTROL]` MRA is the superset invariant ON SILICON** — stock `vsavj` on OUR `.rbf` with byte 41 at the `0xFF` fill. It certifies the BITSTREAM, so run it once per new `.rbf`, not per romset release (recommendation in STATE; the maintainer asked, did not rule). It ships in every release, labelled "run when the bitstream changes".

## 2.5 Field test and triage (`mister_field.md`)

- [MSV-31] **Hardware answers what simulation cannot — pixels seen, voices heard, real SDRAM/timing/analog — and every hardware verdict so far is a person at a CRT.** No frame and no audio has been captured off the board; the field test IS this project's cheapest pixel oracle, and a skill or report that says "verified on hardware" means "judged by eye".
- [MSV-32] **Never field-test without the control**: `[STOCK CONTROL]` on the same `.rbf`. STOCK boots + WIDE fails = OURS (highest-value report); STOCK fails too = NOT ours (bitstream, SDRAM module, card, video chain). Verify the `.rbf` hash and every MRA part BEFORE the card leaves the desk.
- [MSV-33] **Report the failure MODE, and time it**: no sync vs black vs RAM-test loop — a ~26.5 s loop (~1,580 frames at 59.6374 Hz; a phone stopwatch is a valid instrument) is the pre-D5 decryption signature, any other period is a different fault and the number IS the diagnostic; a name-screen reboot after a brief check list = a CPU EXCEPTION (code at `RAM:$FF0000`), the gold full self-test = cold start/watchdog. The download is not comparable (HPS speed).
- [MSV-34] **FIELD REPORTS ARE RECORDINGS** (CLAUDE.md §4, ruled 14z-111): a board crash is captured FIRST as a hand-played MAME `.inp` on the same freeze, named `<what>-<freeze set>-NN`, tracked under `tests/inp/`, replayed by `tools/run_inp_guarded.sh`; `tests/test_inp_corpus.sh` replays every recording at every freeze. Untracked, unnamed recordings are deleted, not kept.
- [MSV-35] **The board is a WITNESS, not the instrument, and a board crash is not evidence about the core.** #99 — 100% on the board AND by hand on MAME — was game DATA (CPU-Phobos playing Demitri's AI through an aliased script table), never the RTL; three sessions of rig-derived mechanism preceded the first recording, which found it in an evening. Reproduce on MAME under the guard before any RTL theory.
- [MSV-36] **The field bundles live OUTSIDE the repo (rule 7) and their READMEs go stale**: a claim corrected in the tree does not reach `../mister_fieldtest_14z11x/`. When a claim is retracted, sweep the artifacts that have already left the tree by hand; `release/<name>/mister/MISTER.md` is the in-tree carrier now.
